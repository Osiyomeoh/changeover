## Things we must NOT claim
- On-device fps or comfort on Quest (not measured: no headset).
- Mixed reality / passthrough / scene understanding / anchors.
- Fresh-eyes tester results until real testers have played.
- That the sound unlocks on Quest Browser: the AudioContext reports `running` in the emulator's Chromium only (no headset).
- How look-to-hold feels with real eye tracking, or that its 0.25 s / 0.15 s / 5° / 8° settings suit real eyes. The emulator's gaze is perfectly steady; real eyes flick and jitter.
- Draw calls or frame rate measured on a headset. Row 10 is the emulator's renderer counters only.
- That a whole night can be played one-handed. Only the changeover has been tested one-handed (row 29).
