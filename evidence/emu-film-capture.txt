and gaze are driven by `xr` CLI commands (animate-to, set-select-value, set-connected, look-at); frames come

## With the game's sound (Nov 16, second capture)
The first cut was picture only (the DevTools screencast carries no sound). All 8 shots were recorded again with
the game's own sound: `python3 scripts/emu-film.py --capture .stills-tmp/film` now opens each shot's page with
CO_AUDIO_TAP=1, and scripts/film-audio-tap.mjs copies the page's audio mix through an AudioWorklet (gain 0
output, so the game sounds the same) with the context frame of every 4096-frame block; getOutputTimestamp()
puts those blocks on the epoch clock the screencast frames carry. film-capture.mjs writes audio.wav per shot.
Nothing is added: no music, no voice, no effects. The game makes every sound with Web Audio (src/sfx.ts).

## Narration (Nov 16)
A narrated version, in a synthetic voice: `python3 scripts/film-cut.py .stills-tmp/film --voice
scripts/film-voice.json` makes the same cut (same frames and game sound) with 33 narrated lines on top:
Amazon Polly, voice Ayanda (South African English; Polly has no Nigerian English voice), generative engine,
called through the aws CLI. Each line is placed `at` seconds into its shot, timed to what is on screen
(sound onsets in each shot's cut sound and 1 fps contact sheets: the trip at 10.9 s into overload, the
refusal at 8.8 s into backfeed, the isolator lighting about 12 s into eyes, the generator at 23.5 s, the
lever springing back about 7 s into look-away). A fit check before the cut found three overlaps and moved
eight lines; none overlap or run past their shot now. The words restate the caption panels and the game's own
text; the numbers are CLAIMS rows 9 and 11.
Mix: the voice alone is brought to -16 LUFS with a limiter at -2 dB on the voice only; the game's sound
keeps its one gain (+13.2 dB) and dips 9 dB under each line (0.25 s ramps); one overall gain keeps the peak
at -1 dB. The closing card adds "The narration is a synthetic voice."
